Momentum signal mechanics on deterministic A-share-shaped data

Research question: Can the research pipeline calculate and rank a 20-bar momentum signal without hidden data dependencies?

Citation

Narasimhan Jegadeesh, Sheridan Titman (1993). Returns to Buying Winners and Selling Losers: Implications for Stock Market Efficiency. DOI:10.1111/j.1540-6261.1993.tb04702.x.

Limitations

  • The five symbols and prices are deterministic synthetic examples, not investable securities history.
  • This validates method plumbing and reproducibility, not the paper's empirical return claim.
  • The 60-day window is too short for statistical inference or transaction-cost conclusions.
