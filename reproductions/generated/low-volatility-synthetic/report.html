Low-volatility ranking mechanics on deterministic A-share-shaped data

Research question: Can the research pipeline measure 20-bar realized volatility and rank a synthetic cross-section without future data?

Citation

Andrew Ang, Robert J. Hodrick, Yuhang Xing, Xiaoyan Zhang (2006). The Cross-Section of Volatility and Expected Returns. DOI:10.1111/j.1540-6261.2006.00836.x.

Method and data

Compute the population volatility of simple close returns over 20 bars and rank low values first.

Limitations

  • The exercise uses total volatility, not the paper's exact idiosyncratic-volatility construction.
  • Synthetic prices cannot support an expected-return claim.
  • No industry neutralization, risk model, portfolio formation or inference is performed.
